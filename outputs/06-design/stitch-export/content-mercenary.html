check_circle Last Verified: August 2026 · Verified

The Mercenary is Mistfall Hunter's frontline bruiser — strong up close, built to hold space and protect teammates.

Mercenary Overview

The Mercenary thrives in the chaos of close-quarters combat. Equipped with heavy armor and devastating melee weaponry, this class excels at disrupting enemy formations and absorbing damage that would otherwise decimate more fragile allies. Their kit is designed around sustain and crowd control, making them an indispensable anchor for any coordinated trio.

Mastering the Mercenary requires a deep understanding of positioning and stamina management. While they possess immense raw power, their mobility is limited. A skilled Mercenary dictates the pace of the engagement, forcing enemies to fight on their terms while providing crucial peeling for their backline damage dealers.
